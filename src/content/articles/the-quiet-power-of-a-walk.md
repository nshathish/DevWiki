A walk is a change of inputs disguised as a break. The body moves, the eye catches peripheral details, and the mind gets a little less interested in defending its first answer.

## Walk with a question

Instead of trying to “clear your head,” take one generous question outside. What am I avoiding? What would make this simpler? What is the smallest honest next step?

Do not force an answer. Notice what your attention chooses when nobody is asking it to be efficient.

## Return with evidence

Bring back one observation, not a conclusion. A single useful detail is often enough to loosen a stuck idea.
