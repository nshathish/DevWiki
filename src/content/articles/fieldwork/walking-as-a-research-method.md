Every place has a rhythm. You can hear it in the distance between doorways, the speed of crossings, and the places where people choose to stop.

## Take the long way once

Choose a familiar destination and remove the most efficient route. Record three things that would have been invisible from a car or a straight line: a repeated sound, an improvised sign, and a place where people gather.

## Return at another hour

A street is not one place. Visit it early, late, and in bad weather. Differences are not noise; they are part of the character of the place.
