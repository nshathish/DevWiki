We often celebrate the moment a system begins and overlook the work that lets it remain trustworthy. Maintenance is not an interruption to the useful part. It is the useful part, repeated over time.

## Make the next action obvious

Every recurring task should end by making its next run easier. Put the checklist where the work happens, name files by what they contain, and leave one sentence about the last decision.

## Reduce the cost of returning

A system is healthy when you can leave it for a month and come back without fear. Remove stale steps, archive what no longer matters, and keep the surface calm enough that the important signal is still visible.
