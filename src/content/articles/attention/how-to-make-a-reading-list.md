A reading list is most useful when it behaves less like a queue and more like a map. It should show you where your curiosity is pointing, not remind you how far behind you are.

## Keep three shelves

Separate the list into three small groups: **now**, **next**, and **someday**. Keep “now” short enough that you can see every title without scrolling. Move things between the shelves whenever your interests change.

The list is allowed to change before you finish anything on it. That is not a lack of discipline; it is evidence that you are paying attention.

## Add a reason

Write one sentence beside every item explaining why it caught you. A title without a reason is easy to postpone. A title attached to a live question has somewhere to land.
